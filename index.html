About me

I'm Shri, an IT student and creative web designer who enjoys combining technology and design to create engaging digital experiences.

I started with curiosity about how pages are put together and stayed for the details: the weight of a heading, the timing of a hover, the moment a layout finally clicks into place on a small screen. I design in the browser, write my own markup and scripts, and care as much about how something feels to use as how it looks in a screenshot.

Right now I'm building interfaces for web projects, sharpening my JavaScript, and looking for internships where I can learn from people who ship real products.
